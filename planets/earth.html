GET TO KNOW EARTH

A Living Planet

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the largest of the four rocky planets in our Solar System. Its surface contains vast oceans, continents, mountains, and deserts.

Around 71% of Earth's surface is covered by liquid water. Its atmosphere contains nitrogen and oxygen, helping regulate temperature and protecting living organisms.

Earth's position, atmosphere, and abundance of liquid water make it an environment where life can survive and develop.

WHAT IS EARTH MADE OF?

Internal Structure

Earth is mainly made of silicate rocks and metals. Its interior is divided into four main layers, each with different physical properties and compositions.

01. Crust

The thin, solid outer layer made mainly of silicate rocks. It forms the continents and ocean floors.

02. Mantle

A thick layer of hot silicate rock that slowly flows over geological timescales.

03. Outer Core

A liquid layer mainly composed of iron and nickel. Its movement helps generate Earth's magnetic field.

04. Inner Core

A solid sphere mainly composed of iron and nickel, kept solid by enormous pressure.

WHAT MAKES EARTH UNIQUE?

The Only Known World with Life

Earth is currently the only planet known to support life. Its liquid-water oceans, suitable temperatures, protective atmosphere, and chemical elements create conditions that allow living organisms to survive.

Scientists continue searching for signs of life beyond Earth, but no confirmed evidence of extraterrestrial life has been discovered.
