A Living Planet

Our Home in Space

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the largest of the four rocky planets in our Solar System. Its surface contains vast oceans, continents, mountains, and deserts.

Approximately 71% of Earth's surface is covered by water. Its atmosphere is mainly composed of nitrogen and oxygen, helping regulate temperature and supporting living organisms.

Earth's suitable temperatures, liquid water, atmosphere, and chemical elements create conditions that allow life to exist.

Earth is the only world where life has been confirmed so far.

Internal Structure

Four main layers make up Earth's interior.

Inside Earth

Earth has four main internal layers. Each layer has a different composition, temperature, and physical state.

From the thin outer crust to the extremely hot inner core, these layers help explain Earth's geological activity and magnetic field.

Crust Thin, solid outer layer made of rock.
Mantle Thick layer of hot silicate rock.

WHAT MAKES EARTH UNIQUE?

The Only Known Planet to Support Life

Earth is currently the only planet known to support life. Its liquid-water oceans, suitable temperatures, protective atmosphere, and available chemical elements provide conditions that allow living organisms to exist.

Scientists continue searching for signs of life beyond Earth, but no extraterrestrial life has been confirmed.
