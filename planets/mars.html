GET TO KNOW THE PLANET

Explore Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and one of Earth's closest planetary neighbours. Its surface contains mountains, valleys, craters, and enormous dust-covered plains.

Its reddish appearance comes mainly from iron minerals in its soil and rocks, which have reacted with oxygen over time. Mars has a thin atmosphere made mostly of carbon dioxide.

Evidence from spacecraft suggests that liquid water once flowed across parts of its surface. Today, water exists mainly as ice, including beneath the surface and at the polar regions.

Did you know?

Mars has seasons because its axis is tilted by approximately 25 degrees, similar to Earth's axial tilt.

WHAT IS INSIDE MARS?

Internal Structure

Mars has a layered internal structure, similar to Earth. It consists of a crust, a rocky mantle, and a metallic core.

Crust

The outer rocky layer, made of volcanic rocks and minerals.

Mantle

A thick layer of hot silicate rock beneath the crust.

Core

A metallic core composed mainly of iron, nickel, and lighter elements.

A REMARKABLE MARTIAN FEATURE

Giants of Mars

Olympus Mons

Mars is home to Olympus Mons, the largest known volcano in the Solar System. It rises approximately 22 kilometres above the surrounding surface, making it nearly three times as tall as Mount Everest.

Its enormous size developed through repeated volcanic eruptions over a long period of time.

EXPLORING THE RED PLANET

Mars Missions

P

Perseverance Rover

NASA's Perseverance rover landed on Mars in February 2021. It explores Jezero Crater, studies Martian rocks and geology, and searches for signs of ancient microbial life.

It also collects and stores rock samples that could be returned to Earth by a future mission.
