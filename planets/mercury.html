01 · PLANET OVERVIEW

A Small World with Extreme Conditions

Mercury is the smallest planet in the Solar System and the closest to the Sun. Its surface is covered with impact craters, making it resemble Earth's Moon.

Unlike Earth, Mercury has an extremely thin exosphere rather than a substantial atmosphere. As a result, its surface experiences enormous temperature differences between day and night.

Mercury also travels around the Sun faster than any other planet, completing one orbit in just 88 Earth days.

Did you know? A year on Mercury lasts only 88 Earth days, meaning it completes more than four orbits around the Sun during one Earth year.

Mercury's ancient surface preserves evidence of billions of years of impacts.

03 · INTERNAL STRUCTURE

What Is Mercury Made Of?

Mercury is a terrestrial planet composed mainly of rocky silicate material and a large metallic core.

A Planet with an Enormous Iron Core

One of Mercury's most unusual characteristics is its exceptionally large metallic core. It occupies a much greater proportion of the planet than Earth's core does.

Scientists believe that Mercury's internal structure consists of three main layers.

1. Crust The outer rocky layer, made mainly of silicate minerals.
2. Mantle A silicate-rich rocky layer beneath the crust.
3. Core A large, iron-rich metallic core, with a liquid outer portion.

04 · UNIQUE SCIENTIFIC DISCOVERY

A Day Longer Than Its Year

Mercury has an unusual relationship between its rotation and its orbit around the Sun. One complete rotation takes approximately 58.6 Earth days, but one complete solar day (sunrise to sunrise) lasts approximately 176 Earth days.

This happens because Mercury rotates three times for every two orbits around the Sun. This relationship is known as a 3:2 spin-orbit resonance.

NASA · MESSENGER MISSION

MESSENGER

NASA's MESSENGER spacecraft became the first spacecraft to orbit Mercury in March 2011. It studied the planet's surface, magnetic field, composition and internal structure.

After more than four years studying Mercury from orbit, the spacecraft completed its mission in April 2015.
