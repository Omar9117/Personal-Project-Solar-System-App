About the Solar System

Our solar system consists of a central star (the Sun), eight planets, five officially recognized dwarf planets, hundreds of moons, and thousands of asteroids and comets, all held together by the Sun's gravity. It formed roughly 4.6 billion years ago from a collapsing cloud of gas and dust (the solar nebula) and is located in the Orion Arm of the Milky Way galaxy.

1 AU (astronomical unit) is the average distance from Earth to the Sun, about 150 million km.
